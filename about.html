About

Founded in 2025, Cofresi Art Handling & Logistics is an artist-run professional fine art services company providing safe handling of artworks for artists, galleries, collectors, and institutions throughout Los Angeles. As a personalized, boutique art handling company, we combine museum-standard practices with direct communication with our clients and individually crafted solutions. From private residences and artists’ studios to galleries and institutions, we are committed to handling every artwork with the gold standard of care, discretion, and professionalism.
